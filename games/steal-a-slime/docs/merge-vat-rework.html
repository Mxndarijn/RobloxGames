Steal a Slime · design review · 22 Oct 2026

Merge vat rework

Merging happens on the vat itself. You Move three slimes of one rarity into the vat, where they sit as small slimes on its three spots. A see-through copy keeps each one's pad. Then you open the vat with E, check what they will make and start a timed brew. You can take a slime back out (X) or cancel the brew while it runs.

How it plays

  1. MoveE Move on a pad slime, as today. No new button. The vat lights up as a target next to your pads.
  2. Place in the vatClick or tap the vat, or E. It takes the next free spot, small. The first one sets the rarity. A full vat says The vat is full.
  3. Manage the vatE Manage vat opens the menu: the three inputs, the result, the brew time.
  4. BrewStart merge locks the inputs and starts the timer. It keeps running while you are offline.
  5. CollectCollect puts the new slime on the first input's pad. The other two pads free up.
  • The pad stays taken. A slime in the vat leaves a see-through copy on its pad, and the pad's sign says In vat. Nothing else can go on that pad.
  • X takes a slime out and sends it back to its own pad. Since that pad was kept for it, X always works.
  • Before the brew starts the copy still earns money as normal, and a thief can still steal it from its pad. The real slime leaves the vat with them and the menu shows the empty spot.
  • Once it brews the copies stay on their pads, but their sign switches to IN VAT and they earn no money. They can't be stolen until you collect.
  • Cancel merge (hold to confirm) stops the brew. Nothing is lost: the three slimes stay in the vat, so you can start again or take them out.
  • The result follows today's rules: a known recipe shows its hybrid by name, everything else shows Random slime with the rarity it will be and the jump chance. An undiscovered recipe still shows as random, so the vat never gives a recipe away.

The screens

All screens use the Goo kit parts: glass panel with the mint band and drips, jelly only on things you can press, Ink wells for states and disabled buttons. Slimes are the kit's stand-in for the 3D previews.

Brew time by rarity

The time depends on the rarity going in. Recipes take the same time as a random merge of that rarity. Results follow today's odds (TripleMerge.odds): one rarity up, 20% chance of two up while that stays at Legendary or below, Mythic is the cap for random results.

Speed-ups

A new upgrade, Quick brew, in the Upgrade shop. Each level takes 15% off every brew, up to 60% at level 4. Like every upgrade it is kept when you rebirth. Each level waits for a rebirth: that needs a small change, since today an upgrade has one rebirth gate for all its levels.

Decided

  • Move puts a slime into the vat (next free spot). A full vat says The vat is full; no swapping, no replacing.
  • Brews keep running while you are offline.
  • Vat slimes keep a see-through copy on their pad. Until the brew starts the copy earns money and can be stolen. While it brews the sign says IN VAT, it earns nothing and it is safe.
  • Collect puts the result on a pad.
  • Speed-ups go in the Upgrade shop behind rebirths (above).
  • Pick for me, Auto-merge and the Keep toggle are gone, with their requests (AutoMerge, KeepSlime, Merge, Merge3). The game isn't live, so nothing needs to stay for old clients.

What changes in code

  • Shared: a pure VatState module (inputs, rarity, start and end time; add, remove, start, cancel, collect, every rule checked before any change). TripleMerge.apply rolls the result at collect.
  • Server: MoveSlime takes the vat as a target (the slime keeps its pad and gets inVat); new requests VatRemove, VatStart, VatCancel, VatCollect; the Manage vat prompt; small slime models on the three spots, see-through copies and In vat signs on the pads; the world tag with the time left.
  • Stealing: stealing an inVat slime before the brew takes it out of the vat; while brewing the steal is refused.
  • Move mode: SlimeMove lights the vat green or red and its bar says why it can't take the slime.
  • Upgrades: QuickBrew in Config.UPGRADES, with a rebirth gate per level.
  • UI: Panels/Merge rebuilt to the screens above; the slime list, filter chips, Pick for me, Auto-merge and Keep go.
  • Removed: MergePlan, the old merge requests and the keep flag on slimes.
  • Save: a migration for the vat and the new upgrade; Lune specs for every rule, then a Studio playtest of the whole loop.
