A new look for the geysers

Today a geyser is hard to read: you can't tell when it will go off. Below are three looks, each shown quiet, about to blow, and erupting. They all keep the same timing. Pick one, A, B or C.

What a geyser does

  • Vent (the orange one). This is a timed spring. It throws you 9 to 14 studs up, but only while it blows: 0.4 to 1.0 s out of every 1.8 to 4 s. The rest of the time it is a plain floor. Today the only warning is its colour turning whiter over the last 0.8 s (BOUNCE_WARN).
  • Red geyser. A red column that is only there while it blows. If you touch it then, you go back to the start pad. When it's off it is see-through and harmless, and it blinks for 0.5 s before it comes back.
  • Pit column. A column out of a red goo pit that you ride up and hover in while it blows. It fades over 0.8 s before it cuts out.
  • One-shot vent. A bubble cap over the vent. Touching it lights a fuse of 0.75 to 1.4 s, then the vent blows once and refills.
  • What is safe. A vent never hurts you: if you miss its blow, you just wait for the next one. Only the red parts hurt. Vents under a red column have a 3-stud strip the red never reaches, which is where you wait.

So the new look has to make three moments obvious: "not yet", "get on now, it's about to go" (the 0.8 s warning) and "it's throwing".

Today

This screenshot is probably not a Geyser Field vent. A dark diamond-plate base, white rings at the top and a yellow frame make up the Updraft wind column (idea 5): a fan base, the wind look's top rings and the glow hover frame. A Geyser Field vent is an orange spring disc with 1 to 3 flat white rings. Its pit column is the same wind look, but with no fan or frame.

Either way, both read as "machine" rather than "geyser", and neither shows the timing well. The options below cover vents, red geysers and pit columns. See the questions at the end about Updraft.

The same in every option

  • Orange still means "throws you" and red still means "hurts" (contract §8, one look per behaviour). The 1 to 3 power rings stay on the vent.
  • The timing doesn't change. The tells look ahead on the same Kit clock the checks use, and the warning stays 0.8 s (Dry Run's fuse is only 0.75 s, so a longer warning would never show in full).
  • Nothing new to upload. Parts and cylinders, CylinderHandleAdornment / SphereHandleAdornment rings and eyes (these add no parts), and ParticleEmitters with built-in textures only: rbxasset://textures/whiteCircle.png (already used by CarryTrail), particles/smoke_main.dds and particles/sparkles_main.dds (both already used by Effects and WeatherFx).
  • No Neon on the column and no Glass. Columns are SmoothPlastic, partly see-through. Only particles get LightEmission.
  • Every new piece is a ghost decor part (no collision, no query, no touch), so it never changes what you stand on or what hurts you. The vent pad itself stays the only thing that throws.
  • Sound is not part of this pick. The existing "boing" stays. A warning rumble would be a separate proposal.
Pick A

Goo Fountain

The vent is a little orange goo pool. When it's about to blow, the goo swells and bubbles hard. Then a thick wobbly goo column shoots up and splashes. This is the most "slime world" option and the closest to a real geyser.

Quiet
A flat orange pool in a darker orange rim, with two or three lazy bubbles.
Warn
The goo domes up, lightens toward white (the glow we have today), shakes, and bubbles come faster and faster.
Blow
A wobbly goo column as tall as the throw, with a splash crown and droplets raining off it. It sinks back as the blow ends.

Build note

  • +2 parts per vent: a Rim cylinder (spring tone 2, a little wider than the pad, its top 0.3 studs over it) and a Column cylinder (spring tone 1, transparency 0.3, hidden at 1 when quiet). ObbyFx sets the column's height every frame: up to the throw height in 0.15 s, then it sinks over the last 30% of the blow.
  • 2 emitters (they add no parts): Bubbles on the pad (whiteCircle, orange tint, Rate 2, ramping to 18 over the warning) and Spray on an attachment at the column top (whiteCircle, Acceleration -40, one Emit(20) when the blow starts, Rate 0 otherwise).
  • Warning: the pad's own colour glow (as today), plus the rim and pad shaking ±0.08 studs, done locally per player.
  • Red geyser: the same goo column in hazard red, keeping the dark edge, plus a red pool part at its foot (+1) that bubbles red during the 0.5 s blink.
  • Pit column: a goo column that stays up while it runs, with bubbles rising inside (one emitter), fading over its 0.8 s.
  • Budget: the busiest section, Red Field (4 vents and 5 red), goes from about 17 to about 30 parts, under the cap of 80. It has 9 emitters in idle, and the busy rates last 0.8 s each.

Risk: an orange column next to the orange spring pads could look like "a spring that grew". Fix: the column only shows while the vent throws, which is exactly what it means.

Pick B

Steam Vent with a closing ring

A stony cone with the orange pad in its mouth. Steam wisps rise from it. When it's about to blow, a white ring appears wide around the vent and closes in. When the ring shuts, a white water column blasts up in a burst of mist. This option shows the timing most exactly: you can watch it count down.

Quiet
A stone cone with one or two thin steam wisps.
Warn
A bright ring appears at about twice the vent's width and shrinks to its middle over the 0.8 s. The steam thickens, the cone rumbles, and the pad glows.
Blow
A white-and-cyan water column, a cloud of mist at its top, and blue-white drops falling around it.

Build note

  • +2 parts per vent: a Cone (a cylinder ring round the pad in a new stone tone, lilac-grey (139, 134, 166), so it is neither a floor colour nor red) and a Column cylinder (white-cyan (234, 249, 255), transparency 0.35, scaled per frame like A's).
  • 1 adornment: the closing ring, a CylinderHandleAdornment whose Radius goes from 2 × the vent radius to 0.5 over the warning. It adds no parts and is the one thing that says "now".
  • 2 emitters: Steam (smoke_main, white, Rate 1 → 8 over the warning) and Mist at the column top (smoke_main and whiteCircle, one Emit(15) when the blow starts).
  • Red geyser: a red column with red steam, and its own closing ring during the 0.5 s blink. Every geyser counts down the same way.
  • Pit column: a water column you ride; the ring closes on it during the 0.8 s fade, meaning "get off".
  • Budget: the same as A, about 30 parts in Red Field. Smoke particles cost a little more fill on phones, so keep them to 8 at most per vent.

Risk: a white column looks like the pale wind look, and steam on stone feels more "volcano park" than goo. It is the least cute of the three.

Pick C

Sleepy Slime

Each vent is a squat orange slime with the pad on its head. It dozes. When it's about to blow, its eyes pop open, it puffs up and shakes. Then it squishes down and spits a goo column. This option has the most character, and the timing has a face: eyes shut means wait, eyes wide means get on now.

Quiet
Eyes shut, slow breathing, little "z" bubbles.
Warn
The eyes snap wide open, the body puffs up about 30%, it shakes, a sweat drop appears, and it lightens toward white.
Blow
It squishes flat with its eyes squeezed shut and spits a goo column with a splash, as in A.

Build note

  • +2 parts per vent: a Body cylinder ring round the pad (spring tone 2, 1.2 studs proud, scaled per frame to breathe, puff and squash) and a Column, as in A.
  • About 6 adornments (they add no parts) for the face on the side you walk up from: two SphereHandleAdornment eye whites and two pupils for "open", and two thin BoxHandleAdornment bars for "shut" and "squeezed". ObbyFx turns them on and off by state.
  • 2 emitters: Doze (whiteCircle, Rate 0.7, quiet only) and Spray at the column top, as in A.
  • Red geyser: a grumpy red slime (angry brows made from two small box adornments) under the red column.
  • Pit column: no face (the pit is red goo). A goo column, as in A.
  • Budget: the same part count as A. It has the most adornments, but those are cheap.

Risk: the face only reads from the side you approach from, and in Quick Spouts and Summit Spouts you often see a vent from above or behind. An orange slime that is not a slime you can steal could also confuse new players. The face also needs the most tuning in Studio.

Recommendation

A, the Goo Fountain, with B's closing ring added. A's goo pool and column fit the slime world best and read from any angle, and its eruption is the clearest "it's throwing" of the three. B's closing ring is one adornment with no part cost, and it turns the warning from "bubbling more" into an exact countdown. C is the most charming, but its face fails from above or behind, which is how you see a lot of these vents.
