Steal a Slime · proposal · 1 Nov 2026

Pick your cloud pet from the SlimeDex

Today the slime on your cloud is always your favourite (or best) slime that you own. This lets you put any slime you have found in the SlimeDex on the cloud, makes the pet a bit bigger, and fixes the jumpy parts of how it flies. Measured in a Studio playtest on latest main.

Decided and built (branch feat/pet-from-dex, not merged)

  1. Picking: option A, a Pet tab in Skins. Auto (your favourite) first, then the slimes you found, then the rest in grey. Looks only; the pet perk stays on your favourite.
  2. Size: 1.5x (pet 2.25 studs, about hip height).
  3. Flight: Merijn didn't want the pet to turn whenever the player turns, but to really move. It now flies on a leash, see below.

How it flies now

  • Turning or spinning on the spot: the pet doesn't move or turn at all (measured 0 studs).
  • Walking away: it is pulled along about 4 studs behind you and drifts to your back-right, facing where it flies.
  • Walking into it: it steps aside and lets you pass, staying at least 2.2 studs away and never swinging faster than 14 studs/s (was 77 in the first build).
  • Jumping: the cloud stays at the height you jumped from (it was rising 5.6 studs).

Part 3

Smooth flight: what I measured

I walked the avatar through four moves in a hidden Studio playtest and logged the pet every frame (about 60 per second): walk straight, jump, turn around on the spot, and stop-and-go. Then I ran the same moves with a prototype of the fix. Walking and stopping are already smooth: no pops, no rebuilds. Three things are not.

How it would work

Build outline

  • Save: new petSpecies (a species id, or empty = Auto). No version bump; an unknown or not-found species loads as Auto.
  • Server: request SetPetSpecies, refused unless the species is found in your Dex. Player attribute PetSpecies next to PetSlimeId.
  • Client (FollowPets): with a species set, build the pet from that species' model (the same kit mesh the SlimeDex shows), not from a slime on your plot. Other players' picked species load the same way.
  • Size and flight: PetCloud.PET_SIZE 1.5 → 2.25 (1.5x) with a matching hover height; the three motion fixes in PetFollow.
  • Contract: update contract/pet-seats.md and the follow-pet sections; Decision log row once you pick.
