Steal a Slime · proposal · 2026-10-31

21 new slimes, Legendary and up

18 new wild slimes that drop from the fountain like every other slime, plus 3 merge-only slimes: 7 Legendary, 7 Mythic, 3 Secret, 2 Celestial and 2 Divine. Each one earns about the average of its rarity, so income per rarity barely moves. The open question is what they do to the slimes rebirths ask for.

Decided

  • Normal wild slimes: they spawn from the fountain, can be stolen, merged, shined and grown like any other slime, and come out of random merges. No variants, no featured weeks. (Merijn, 2026-10-31)
  • SlimeDex: bonus entries (option 1): they show in the Dex, but the Dex total stays 50, so Dex milestones, Dex badges and the Rainbow cloud (80%) don't move. (Merijn, 2026-10-31)
  • Count: one less Celestial and Divine than the first draft, two more Legendary and Mythic. Comet and Lotus Sage are dropped; Cowboy, Astronaut, Cerberus and Djinn are added. (Merijn, 2026-10-31)
  • Merge-only: one Secret, one Celestial and one Divine never drop wild; they only come out of the vat from their recipe (section 2). Picked: Hydra, Constellation and Ryujin. (Merijn asked for one per rarity, 2026-10-31; which three is the bot's pick)
  • Merge-only income: like every other hybrid (M2): ×1.2 hybrid bonus times the vat ×2 = ×2.4. (Merijn, 2026-10-31)
  • Rebirths: one Celestial at 24 (Lunara) and 28 (Solaris), Aurora at 29, Goo Oracle and Kirin at 30. (Merijn, 2026-10-31)
  • Still open: whether the new wild slimes should only take drops from slimes no rebirth asks for (X), and whether rebirth 30 keeps two Divines. The pacing sim shows grinders about 25% slower to rebirth 30, and a third not reaching it within 55 days.
  • Models: all 21 approved on the model review page after a second pass on Monarch, Djinn, Constellation and Valkyrie. (Merijn, 2026-10-31)

1 · The slimes

The set decides when a slime starts dropping: Meadow from the start, Garden from rebirth 3, Frost & Sea from 6, Starlight from 10. Income is the species bonus within its rarity (×1 = the rarity's base), set to the average of the slimes a player has at that point.

2 · Merge-only slimes

A merge-only slime is a recipe hybrid (kind = "hybrid"), like Dragon King or Nightwing: three set species of one rarity in the vat give it, one rarity up. It never drops from the fountain or a random merge, so it doesn't touch the wild odds. Recipe inputs avoid every slime rebirths ask for.

SlimeRecipeDex hint
Hydra SecretSiren + Siren + Siren (Mythic)"Three sirens, one tail."
Constellation CelestialGaia + Abyss + Monarch (Secret)"Earth, sea and wings, joined by starlight."
Ryujin DivineLunara + Solaris + Supernova (Celestial)"Moon, sun and a bursting star: a dragon wakes."
  • Ryujin is the first recipe that takes Celestials. Three Celestials can't merge today (MERGE_MAX_TIER = 8: tier 8 and up only merge in a recipe), so it gives top players a new use for spare Celestials.
  • The vat checks recipes before the shine recipe. Three Gold Gaia/Abyss/Monarch would make Constellation instead of a Divine. Fix: let the shine recipe win when all three share a shine.

Their income: decided M2

M1 · not picked

Same as a wild slime of their rarity (×1.0)

Their species bonus is set to ×0.5, so with the vat bonus (RECIPE_INCOME_MULT ×2) they earn ×1.0. Three slimes in, one of the next rarity out: about what a random merge gives today. No economy change.

M2 · picked

Like every other hybrid (×2.4)

The default hybrid bonus ×1.2 times the vat ×2. Ryujin would earn about 19,200 a second against 7,500 for the three Celestials that went in. Consistent with the other 15 hybrids, but a clear income jump at the top end.

3 · Open: the slimes rebirths ask for

The 18 wild slimes change the odds. Within a rarity, every species shares the drops (Genome.speciesWeight = 1 / income⁴). More species means each existing one drops less often, including the ones Config.REBIRTH_SLIMES asks for. Income per rarity stays within 4%, but rebirths that need King, Cosmo or Midas take 2–2.5× as many drops of that rarity.

X · recommended

Rebirth slimes keep their odds

The new slimes still drop wild from the fountain, but their share comes only from slimes no rebirth asks for (Sir Goo, Sunny, Captain Goo, Phoenix, Elder Oak, Yeti, Gaia, Abyss). King, Cosmo, Midas and the rest keep exactly today's odds. Players can't tell the difference: it is only how the drop is weighted.

Y

Plain odds, retune rebirths

Every species shares equally, as in the table. Then re-run the pacing sim (docs/economy-sim) and change REBIRTH_SLIMES or SPECIES_RARITY_POWER so a 30-rebirth run still takes about 150–170 hours, as decided in #561.

Z

Plain odds, accept slower rebirths

Ship as in the table. Rebirths 5–30 get noticeably slower for players who wait for a drop; stealing an asked slime is unchanged.

5 · Cost and order
