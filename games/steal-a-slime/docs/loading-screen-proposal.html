A better loading screen

Steal a Slime's join screen works, but it sits still for up to 25 seconds and has no slime on it. Three options to make it feel alive, from small to big.

Decided B

Option B, slime rides the bar. Merijn: "Build B", 2026-11-06. The five draft tips below ship as written until he changes them.

Built with a gold slime (Goo palette Gold) and the bar 24 px lower than now, so the hopping slime clears the title.

What's wrong now

  • Nothing moves. On a slow phone it looks frozen, and players leave.
  • The bar jumps. 0 → 20% → 50%, then it creeps and sits at 99%.
  • The text is technical. "Building the world...", "Loading textures..."
  • No slime in a game called Steal a Slime.

The look itself (teal, mint goo, title) is the Goo kit and stays in every option.

The options

A. Living goo small

Same screen, but it moves: the drips stretch and sway, a drop falls now and then, bubbles rise, and a shine runs along the bar. The bar slides smoothly instead of jumping.

No new pictures or uploads. About half a day.

B. Slime rides the bar recommended

Everything in A, plus a little slime hopping along the tip of the bar. The status line becomes one short tip that changes every 4 seconds.

The slime is drawn from Goo kit shapes, so it shows from the first frame, before any image could load. Its colour could be a random common slime each join.

No uploads. About a day. Tip wording needs your OK.

C. Peek at the ranch big

Starts like B. Once the world has arrived, the teal opens into a slow camera pan over the ranch, inside a goo frame, with the bar at the bottom. It ends on your own base.

Most exciting, but the pan can show meshes that haven't loaded yet, which is the thing this screen exists to hide. Needs a camera hand-off to the player. Two to three days and a Studio check on a slow connection.

Fixes in every option

  • The bar glides to each new value and keeps creeping slowly, so it never looks stuck.
  • Friendly labels instead of "Loading textures..." (A keeps one label, B and C use tips), "Ready!" at the end.
  • Motion stops when Roblox's Reduced Motion setting is on (the game's own setting isn't loaded yet at this point).
  • Same load rules as now: 25 s at most, Skip after 6 s, 1 s minimum.
