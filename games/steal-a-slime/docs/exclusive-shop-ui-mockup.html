Exclusive shop: UI directions

Design mockup, nothing is built. Three layouts, each on desktop, phone (portrait, 390 px wide) and phone landscape (844 × 332, under the top bar). Prices are placeholders.

From the Skins menu. In the Skins mockup an Exclusive cloud shows a gold "Exclusive shop" well. That becomes a real button: it closes Skins and opens this shop with that cloud already selected, so the player lands on its Buy button.

From the world. Walking up to the carousel and pressing its prompt opens the shop on its first tab.

Back again. After buying, the "Bought" state says the cloud is now in Skins → Cloud, and Equip works right here, so nobody has to go looking.
The carousel's lilac, plum, butter stripes and gold are used as accents (the star tile, the stage behind the preview, Direction B's canopy). The panel itself stays Goo so the shop feels like the rest of the game.

A. Goo counter

The Skins menu's layout, turned into a shop: tabs on top, a grid of cards, a detail pane on the right. On a phone the detail slides up as a sheet.

Every screen is clickable: pick a cloud, press Buy, then the prompt's Buy or Cancel.

B. Carousel stage

The shop looks like the ride outside: a striped canopy with gold scallops, the selected cloud on a turning stage under a gold pole, and the whole range as round "seats" along the bottom. Arrows flip through it one by one.

C. Quick list

A list of rows with the price button on every row: one tap to Roblox's prompt. Tapping the row itself opens a short detail card. Categories live in a rail on the left (a tab row on phone).

Same in all three

  • Roblox's prompt is the confirm step. Every Robux purchase goes through Roblox's own prompt, which we can't style. A second confirm of our own before it would just be an extra tap, so there is none. While the prompt is open and until the server grants the cloud, our button is a sunken "Waiting for Roblox…" well. Cancel puts the player back where they were, with no message.
  • States: for sale shows the price ("R$ 199"), owned shows a sunken "Owned" well and an Equip button in the detail, equipped shows a mint "Equipped" well and a mint outline. States are wells, not buttons, as the styleguide says.
  • Buy is mint (the main action), not gold. Gold stays for money and the "Exclusive" tag. Inside the shop the cards drop the Exclusive tag (everything here is exclusive); the detail keeps it so it matches the Skins menu.
  • Wording: "Buy R$ 199" → "Bought". No "!" on buttons, sentence case, the chip counts "2/16 owned".
  • More categories: the dashed "room for more" outlines are only notes in this mockup. Until there is a second category the tab row shows just "Clouds", with no "Coming soon".
  • Descriptions are first drafts (one cozy sentence each). Whether each cloud is a game pass or a developer product is an engineering choice; the UI is the same either way.

Recommendation: A, with B's stage behind the preview

Build A (Goo counter). It is the Skins menu players already know, it reuses that panel's code, it shows all 16 clouds at once, and new categories are just another tab. Take one thing from B: the plum stage behind the big preview (already in A's pane above), so the shop has the carousel's colours without a new kind of header. Keep C's idea for later: if the catalogue grows to many cheap items, a price button on each card would save a tap.

B is the most fun to look at but costs the most and fits perks and boosts less well; it could come back as a "featured" banner on top of A if the shop ever runs limited-time items.
