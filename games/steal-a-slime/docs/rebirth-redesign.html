What changes

  • Reward first. A big "×3.5 ➜ ×4.0" replaces the two small "income" and "lock time" tiles under "You get:". Lock time moves to a chip next to it.
  • One checklist. Cash and every slime are rows of the same shape, with a "1 of 3 ready" counter. Today one slime sits beside the cash card and several slimes switch to a two-column grid; now 1, 2 or 3 slimes just add rows.
  • Rank shows up. A gold-ringed row when this rebirth earns a rank (title, income bonus, unlocked upgrade or new slime family), otherwise a quiet "Next rank at Rebirth 30" row. The panel never mentioned ranks before.
  • Keep and resets side by side replace the "Keeps your other slimes" line and the fine-print paragraph.
  • Status reads at a glance: mint tick when met, pink "Missing" chip when not, gold "$9.2M" on the cash row while you save up. The bottom well still names what's missing.
  • Maxed gets its own screen (rank, totals) instead of an empty panel with one well.

Unchanged

  • Goo look and colour roles from the styleguide: mint for the action and income, gold for money and the rank reward, pink for missing.
  • Hold to confirm, the real 3D slime previews (greyed when missing), and the "Need $6.3M and a Flan" wording.
  • Same server data. The only new inputs are the current multiplier and the rank row, both from Config and Perks already on the client.
  • Scrolls on landscape phones like today; the panel is about 30 px taller with three slimes.

Your call

  1. Keep the "You keep / Resets" boxes, or drop them for a shorter panel?
  2. On the missing rows, a hint on where to get it (e.g. "Merge Bumble + Froggo") instead of "Catch or merge one"? It needs a lookup in the merge recipes.
  3. The rank row's text: "Frost slimes start showing up" or the shorter "Unlocks Frost family"?
