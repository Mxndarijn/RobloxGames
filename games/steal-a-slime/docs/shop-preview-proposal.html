Shop previews and item looks

Steal a Slime, Exclusive Shop. A player should see what they buy before they pay, and no item should look like a placeholder. This page has the audit, three ways to preview, and the list of items to upgrade. Merijn picks; nothing is uploaded or shipped before that.

Decided

Not decided yet. Pick one preview option (A, B or C) and tick the upgrades you want. Built on branch docs/shop-preview-audit behind the recommended pick (A) so it can be tried in Studio; it changes nothing that is uploaded.

What players see today

The shop's right-hand pane shows the card's icon again, only bigger. There is no 3D view and no way to see a hat on a slime, a lock style on a door, or what a bundle holds. Half the Style tab is placeholders.

Yet the real models are already uploaded and most of them look good:

The white orbs

Three things show up as plain white or lilac orbs:

  • The claw prize. After any purchase that is not a featured cloud (hats, lock styles, skins, bases, Bat skins, trails, every pass) the claw grabs a plain lilac ball (ShopDisplayFx.makePrize).
  • The capsules in the box are part of the claw shop mesh: untextured pastel balls.
  • The Bubble cloud mesh itself is a pale flattened orb.

How the preview could work

Each mockup is the shop's right-hand pane (on a phone it is the bottom sheet). Same kit: plum stage, butter turntable, mint main button.

A. The model turns on the stage Recommended

Exclusive shop✕
Top Hat

Every slime on your pads wears it.

The medal on the stage is swapped for the real 3D model, slowly turning on the turntable. Hats sit on a slime, clouds carry a slime, lock styles stand in a door ring, Bat skins and skin decor turn on their own. Bundles show their items side by side on the turntable.

For: no new buttons or screens; uses the uploaded meshes, so no upload; works the same on a phone. Against: the stage is small (about 220 × 150 px); plot bases are whole buildings and stay a picture.

B. A big preview you can spin

Top Hat✕
Drag to turn
Buy · R$99

A secondary Preview button in the pane opens the whole panel as one big stage. Drag to turn it; for hats and clouds, your own favourite slime wears or rides it. X goes back to the list.

For: big enough to see details; your own slime sells it. Against: one more button and one more screen per item; drag needs care next to the scroll list on a phone.

C. Try it on in the world

Trying: Space Rocket 0:12

A Try it button closes the shop and shows the item for 15 seconds where it belongs, for you only: the base on your plot, the hat on your pad slimes, the trail behind you, the cloud under your pet. The camera flies there and back.

For: the only option that shows plot bases and trails for real. Against: the most work and the most edge cases (stealing, other players, the camera); you leave the shop to see it.

What was checked, and what was not

  • Checked in a hidden, muted Studio playtest: every shop icon (drawn by the game's own code), every cosmetic mesh loading on the client, the claw machine from the front.
  • Not checked: purchases with real Robux (none are made from a bot), a real phone (only emulated sizes), the claw show playing with a new prize.
