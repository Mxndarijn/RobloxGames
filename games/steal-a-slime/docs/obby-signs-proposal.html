Picture signs for the obby

Signs that explain the unclear sections with pictures only: no words, at most an arrow or a number. 15 mechanics get a sign, 10 are skipped because they explain themselves. Every sign is shown in three styles; pick a style (A, B or C) and which signs you want.

What you pick: 1) one style for all signs, 2) which mechanics get a sign (the "Needed" ones, or also the "Nice to have"), 3) whether the old text signs (FAST, LOW GRAVITY, ...) become pictures too.

Nothing needs an upload. Every pictogram is built in code from Frames, UICorner (circles, pills), UIStroke (outlines, rings), rotated Frames (arrows, chevrons, cracks) and a few TextLabel glyphs (▲ ★ ⚡ ⌛). Curves are chains of round dots, like the cannon's aim line already is.

The three styles

Read distance on a phone (landscape, 390 px tall): one stud at 26 studs covers about 10.7 px (guides/roblox-world-labels.md). A shape is spotted at 6 px and read at 14 px, so every main shape in a pictogram is at least 1.3 studs on the sign and outlines are at least 0.5 stud.

Each style is a new world label, so the build PR adds its row to the label registry in guides/roblox-world-labels.md.

Recommendation: A, the board on a post. It reads from 26 studs on a phone, faces the runner before the section starts, sits where the text boards already stand (so it's one swap in ObbyParts), and is one look everywhere. B is too flat to read until you're on the pad. C reads well, but a bubble over every hazard is more on screen while you play; it's worth it for the cannon only (the four-step bubble over the barrel), as an extra on top of A.

How to use the cannon

From AimTheCannon.luau and the cannon controller in ObbyMoves.luau: you walk into the barrel (from behind, within 1.5 studs of it) and snap in; the camera moves behind the barrel. You don't aim: the barrel sweeps left and right by itself, and a dotted line shows where the shot lands right now: mint on a target, red when it would miss or hit goo. Jump fires (a press in the first 0.2 s doesn't count). You fly the arc and land on the island with the gold star. After a shot the same cannon won't take you for 1 s.

1 · Walk inWalk into the barrel from behind.
2 · It swingsThe barrel swings by itself. Red dots: not yet.
3 · Jump to fireDots are mint and end on the star: press jump.
4 · LandYou fly the arc and land on the star.

The step numbers are the only marks on the sign. In style A it's one wide board (12 × 3.5 studs, each panel 3 studs: readable from about 15 studs, which is the length of the cannon pad). In B it's four tiles on the floor leading up to the barrel. In C the bubble over the barrel steps through the four panels, one second each, and hides while you're seated so it never covers the aim dots.

The signs

One card per mechanic. The square on the left is the pictogram itself; the three pictures show it in each style. "How it's built" lists the GuiObjects for each one.

Idea 26 Needed

Aim the Cannon

Nothing tells you to walk into the barrel from behind, that the barrel swings by itself (you don't steer it), or that Jump fires. The dots go mint when the shot lands on a star and red when it misses: that's the only aim hint, and it only shows once you're seated.

The picture says: Walk into the barrel → it swings by itself → jump when the dots are mint → land on the star.

How it's built (no upload)

A: a wide board (12 × 3.5 studs) with the four steps of the strip below, one Frame cell each. Barrel = rounded Frame + orange band Frame, rotated 25-30°. Dots = 10 round Frames (UICorner 1,0) placed on the arc in code. Jump button = round Frame with UIStroke + a rotated-bar chevron (2 Frames). Star = a TextLabel ★ glyph in gold, or the same two rotated squares as the existing star marker. About 22 GuiObjects per panel.

C: the bubble floats over the barrel and steps through the 4 panels, one second each (4 Frames, Visible toggled by ObbyFx's clock). Hide it while you're seated, so it never covers the aim dots.

Idea 14 Needed

Rolling Blobs

Red balls drop out of a dark launcher and roll down at you. New players walk straight into the first one: there's no hint to step to the side or wait for a gap.

The picture says: Red balls roll down at you → step aside.

How it's built (no upload)

Road = 2 rotated thin Frames + the top bar. Ball = round Frame (UICorner 1,0), red, UIStroke dark red 4 px + a small light round Frame for shine. Motion lines = 3 thin Frames. Figure = head circle + capsule body (2 Frames). Curved arrow = 5 small dots + a rotated triangle (TextLabel ▲ or 2 rotated bars). About 16 GuiObjects.

C: over the launcher's mouth at the top of the slope.

Idea 8 Needed

Climb (red drips)

Red drops swell under a nozzle and fall down the ladder. It looks like decoration until one sends you back. You also can't tell that only the green lattice can be climbed.

The picture says: Red drops fall down the ladder → wait for the drop to pass, then climb.

How it's built (no upload)

Nozzle = grey Frame with UIStroke. Ladder = 2 rails + 4 rungs (6 green Frames). Drop = round Frame + a rotated square on top (makes the teardrop point), red with a dark UIStroke. Down arrow = bar Frame + triangle. Figure = 2 Frames. About 14 GuiObjects.

C: at the top of each ladder, beside the nozzle (not in front of it).

Idea 10 Needed

Glow Memory

The safe path only glows for a moment, then every tile looks the same. Players who look away miss the glow and don't know that the plain tiles crack. The current text sign "Remember the glow" uses words.

The picture says: Watch (eye) → the glowing tiles are the path → the other tiles crack.

How it's built (no upload)

Eye = 1 Frame (rotated 45° square with UICorner gives an almond) + round pupil Frame. Grid = 9 rounded Frames; the 3 path tiles are gold with a bigger see-through gold Frame behind for glow. Crack = 3 short rotated red bars. About 17 GuiObjects. Can even blink the gold tiles in step with the real glow (same clock).

C: over the back of the entry pad, facing the grid.

Idea 18 Needed

Beat Pads

Pads shrink and grow on the beat, and you have to jump when the next pad is at full size. Without a hint it feels random: players jump too early and blame the jump.

The picture says: Count the beat (three dots, the last is gold) → jump on the gold beat from full pad to full pad.

How it's built (no upload)

Beat dots = 3 round Frames; the gold one can pulse with Kit.BPM (same clock as the pads). Pads = 2 flat round Frames (UICorner 1,0 on a 40 × 14 Frame) + a dashed small ring (UIStroke on a see-through Frame: dashes aren't possible, so a thin solid ring). Arc = 9 dots + triangle. Figure = 2 Frames. About 18 GuiObjects.

C: over the first moving pad, pulsing with the beat.

Idea 20 Needed

Key Run

The gate looks like a wall. Players don't link the floating gold keys off the path to the lock icons on the gate, so they try to get past the gate or turn back.

The picture says: Grab the gold keys → the gate opens.

How it's built (no upload)

Key = ring Frame (UIStroke gold, see-through fill) + shaft Frame + 2 teeth Frames. Arrow = bar + triangle. Gate = see-through violet Frame with UIStroke. Open lock = gold body Frame + an open shackle (a ring Frame cropped by a ClipsDescendants Frame, or two bars). About 11 GuiObjects. Could show the real lock count (1-3 small locks) from the gate's need.

C: over the gate itself (it already has lock icons, so the bubble only shows key → open lock).

Idea 29 Needed

Clue Doors

Three curtains with symbols and a board with a symbol above them: nothing says that the board is the clue and you walk through the matching door. A wrong door is a trapdoor, so a guess feels unfair.

The picture says: The board's symbol → walk through the door with the same symbol.

How it's built (no upload)

Board = see-through Frame with UIStroke. Symbols: circle = round Frame, square = Frame, triangle = TextLabel ▲ glyph (or the slime emoji silhouette for the slime clue). Doors = 3 Frames with only top corners rounded (a UICorner Frame plus a covering Frame for the bottom). Curved arrow = dots + triangle. About 15 GuiObjects. The symbols are generic: the sign explains the rule, not this room's answer.

C: over the clue board, pointing down at it.

Idea 22 Needed

Sticky Walls

The gap is too wide to jump, and nothing says that the dripping green walls catch you in the air and that Jump throws you off them.

The picture says: Jump at the green wall → you stick → jump again to throw yourself to the next wall.

How it's built (no upload)

Walls = 2 lime Frames + 4 small drip Frames (rotated squares). Handprint = palm circle + 3 finger bars (4 Frames, the same handprint as the in-world marker). Figure = 2 Frames. Slide-down = 2 short dashed bars. Arc = 8 dots + triangle. About 20 GuiObjects.

C: over the first sticky wall.

Idea 28 Needed

Moon Jump

The bubble dome is see-through and looks like decoration. Players don't try the far jumps because they don't know their jump floats higher inside it. The current sign says "LOW GRAVITY" in words.

The picture says: Inside the bubble (moon) your jump goes high and far.

How it's built (no upload)

Dome = a big round Frame cropped to its top half by a ClipsDescendants Frame, see-through lilac with UIStroke. Moon = gold round Frame with a board-coloured round Frame over its side (crescent). Up arrow = bar + triangle. High platform = bar. Arc = 10 dots. Figure = 2 Frames. About 18 GuiObjects.

C: inside the first bubble, up high.

Idea 16 Needed

Speed Strip (back strips)

Yellow strips speed you up; the dusky blue back strips slow you. Most players never notice the second kind and miss the gap without knowing why.

The picture says: Yellow = fast (lightning). Blue = slow (snail).

How it's built (no upload)

Strips = 2 rounded Frames. Chevrons = 6 pairs of rotated bars (12 Frames), the same chevrons the strips already wear. Lightning = TextLabel ⚡ glyph (emoji) or 3 rotated bars. Snail = a round Frame with UIStroke (shell) + a flat body Frame + 1 bar for the feeler. About 20 GuiObjects.

C: over the first back strip only (the yellow ones are clear on their own).

Idea 23 Needed

Arrow Maze (belts)

Belt cells carry you the way their arrow points, and pink cells flip their direction. Without a hint players think the floor is broken, or walk against a belt.

The picture says: The floor carries you the arrow's way → pink tiles switch direction.

How it's built (no upload)

Tiles = 2 rounded Frames (orange, pink). Arrows = bar Frames + triangle heads (TextLabel ▶ or 2 rotated bars). Speed lines = 2 bars. Figure = 2 Frames. About 14 GuiObjects.

C: over the first pink (turning) cell, or over the first belt row if the variant has none.

Idea 17 Needed

Lights Out

The screen goes dark and only a small light shows round you. Players don't know that the glowing green things are the safe places to jump to.

The picture says: In the dark, jump to the glowing green.

How it's built (no upload)

Night panel = near-black Frame. Moon = crescent (2 round Frames). Your light = a big see-through warm round Frame. Green slime = a round-topped Frame (UICorner on top only, via a covering Frame) + a see-through green glow circle + a platform bar. Arc = 7 dots + triangle. Figure = 2 Frames. About 15 GuiObjects. Set LightInfluence 0 so it stays bright in the dark zone.

C: at the section entry, before the dark starts (inside the dark it would be the brightest thing and pull the eye).

Idea 15 Nice to have

Vine Swing

You have to jump at the knob to grab it, then press Jump to let go. The only hint is the text toast "Jump to let go" on the first 3 grabs, and nothing explains the grab.

The picture says: Jump to the green knob → swing → press jump to let go.

How it's built (no upload)

Rail = 1 bar. Rope = 1 rotated bar Frame. Knob = round Frame. Swing arc = 8 dots (dashed look). Release arrow = rotated bar + triangle. Jump button = round Frame with UIStroke + chevron (3 Frames), the same as the cannon's. Figure = 2 Frames. About 17 GuiObjects.

C: over the first rope's rail. Could later replace the "Jump to let go" text toast (a separate question).

Idea 21 Nice to have

Geyser Field

Orange vents only throw you while their rim glows. Standing on a dead vent does nothing, so it reads as a broken spring pad.

The picture says: Dead vent: wait (hourglass). Glowing rim: it throws you up.

How it's built (no upload)

Divider = 4 short bars. Vents = 2 flat round Frames; the lit one gets a gold UIStroke ring + 2 ray bars. Hourglass = TextLabel ⌛ glyph (emoji), or 2 rotated squares. Up arrow = bar + triangle. Figures = 4 Frames. About 16 GuiObjects.

C: over the first timed vent.

Idea 5 Nice to have

Updraft

The wind column is very see-through (transparency 0.85) and floats 3.5 studs over the pad behind a kerb. Players walk up to the kerb and stop: they don't see that you jump into the wind.

The picture says: Jump into the wind column → it carries you up.

How it's built (no upload)

Column = see-through pale-blue Frame with UIStroke. Fan = grey bar. Swirls = 3 short arcs (use small rotated bars or a TextLabel ~ glyph). Up arrow = bar + triangle. Ledge = bar. Arc = 6 dots. 2 figures = 4 Frames. About 17 GuiObjects.

C: over the first column, at hover height.
