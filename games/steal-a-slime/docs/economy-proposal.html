Slime Economy Plan

Today the early rarities are gone within five minutes and rebirths hit a wall halfway. This plan slows the start, makes every rebirth reachable, gives merging a real payoff and keeps every number under $1.7T, with 30 rebirths as the top. Every number below comes from the game's own pacing simulator running the real server code, with this plan built into it: 20 servers for one week, plus 4 servers for 55 days to reach the late game.

What is wrong today

  • PaceCommon and Uncommon last about one minute.An average player owns an Epic after 1 minute and a Legendary after 5. Three things cause it: every wild slime costs only 20 seconds of its own income (a Legendary is about $1,800), the launch codes hand out $6,000 at once, and the first goals pay more than the first ten minutes of income (Unlock 6 pads pays $500 for pads that cost $350).
  • WallRebirths stop around 16 to 19.Cost grows x4.5, then x2.25 per rebirth, while income grows by +0.5x per rebirth. A grinder who plays 224 hours reaches rebirth 19. Rebirth 30 costs $2.3 × 10¹⁶, rebirth 50 $1.2 × 10²³.
  • BugRebirths 41, 43 and 49 can never be done.They ask for Goozilla, Queen Jelly and Mecha Goo. Beating a boss gives a badge and a Dex entry, never a slime on your pads, so the real cap is 40. In the 55-day run, every grinder stopped at exactly 40 until the list was fixed.
  • RebirthRebirth slimes are about two rarities below what players own.Rebirth 1 asks for an Uncommon Bumble when the plot is full of Epics, rebirth 10 asks for an Epic when the plot is Legendary and Secret. Players have already sold those slimes, and a full plot can't grab a wild slime, so they must sell something good to fetch something worthless.
  • MergeMerging barely pays.Three slimes in, one out, of the next rarity: the income is the same plus 14% from the size bonus. A random Mythic x3 merge gives one Mythic back, a 62% income loss. Secret x3 loses too.
  • ExploitSelling pays more than buying.A wild slime costs 20 seconds of base income; selling it pays 30 seconds of income times the rebirth multiplier. Buying a slime and selling it straight away always makes money.
  • SinksNothing to buy after rebirth 6.Every upgrade is maxed by then (the most expensive level is $8M). Late cash only goes to pads and rebirths.
  • IdleOffline income is most of all income.60 to 77% of all cash is earned while offline (50% rate for up to 2 hours).
  • ToolThe pacing simulator underestimated the speed.Its bots never sold a slime before walking to the fountain, but the game refuses a grab with a full plot, so full bots stalled. With that fixed, today's game is faster than the October baselines showed. The fix is two small changes in tools/livetest/pacing/Bot.luau.

Early game: time in every rarity

The target: the first ten minutes are Commons and Uncommons with a first Rare, Epics arrive around ten minutes, the first Legendary takes most of the first session, and Mythic and Secret stay a chase for days. Price does most of the work: a slime now pays itself back slower the rarer it is, so a Legendary is a real saving goal instead of a 2-minute purchase.

Wild slime prices

Price = the slime's base income x its size x the payback seconds of its rarity. The rebirth multiplier doesn't count, so for a player at rebirth 10 the same slime pays back about 7 times faster: the fountain gets cheap late, and late players compete for luck instead of cash. Rebirths

Rebirth keeps working the way it does now: cash goes back to $25, the required slimes are used up, and every other slime and every pad stays. What changes is the price list, the income multiplier, and what each rebirth asks for and opens. The aim: a grinder reaches the top, rebirth 30, in about 150 to 170 played hours (about 6 weeks at 4 hours a day), so new content can arrive before the best players run out.

Bigger multiplier steps late

Today every rebirth adds +0.5x. At rebirth 25 that takes income from x13 to x13.5, about +4%, which feels like nothing for hours of saving. The proposal grows the step: +0.5x for rebirths 1 to 10, +1x for 11 to 20, +1.5x for 21 to 30. A late rebirth now adds about +8%, and the top is x31 instead of x16. The cost table was calibrated with this multiplier.

Cost, slimes and unlocks per rebirth

How the cost table was made: start from a guess, run the 55-day simulation, compare the median hours to each rebirth with a target pace (rebirth 1 at 30 min, 5 at 2.2 h, 10 at 7 h, 15 at 18 h, 20 at 42 h, 25 at 90 h, 30 at 150 h), rescale each cost by how far off it was, and repeat. Two rounds with the new multiplier and upgrades got every level within about 10% of target; the table was then smoothed and rounded, and the final setup was simulated once more.

"Income x" is the rebirth multiplier, today → proposed. Rank income bonuses stay as they are (+5% at 6 up to +200% at 30), plus the new Goo Titan rank at 25 (+150%), which also fills the gap between 20 and 30. A title, plot skin or aura for ranks 20, 25 and 30 would make them feel like milestones.

Merge vat

  • One rarity per rank. Rookies merge Commons only. Uncommon opens at rebirth 1, Rare at 3, Epic at 5, Legendary at 8, Mythic at 12 (for the Nightwing recipe only); no rank opens Secret. Each rank brings the next step on the ladder instead of two at once.
  • Brewed slimes earn x1.5, recipe slimes x2. The bonus is part of the slime, shows as a badge, and travels with it when it is stolen (which makes brewed slimes good targets). It does not stack: merging brewed slimes gives a brewed slime, still x1.5.
  • Longer brews. 1, 3, 7, 15, 30, 60 and 90 minutes by the rarity going in (today 0.5 to 30 min). Quick Brew still takes 15% off per level.
  • No merges that lose income. A random Mythic x3 or Secret x3 merge is refused with "Only a recipe works here"; Nightwing and the other recipes still work. The vat preview shows the income change before the brew starts.

Upgrades

Today the most expensive upgrade level costs $8M and everything is maxed by rebirth 6, so from then on cash only buys pads and rebirths. The proposal keeps the five upgrades and stretches the two that matter most for income, with levels that open at rebirths, so there is always a next level to save for between rebirths.

Smaller changes

Pads

Today pads are bought with cash only, and their price falls far behind: the last pad costs $6.7B while a late rebirth costs hundreds of billions, so players buy pads without noticing. The proposal ties every pad to a rebirth and prices it from the rebirth curve:

  • Which pad opens when. Four pads to start, pads 5–8 for cash ($250, $1K, $4K, $12K). After that each rebirth from 1 to 18 opens one more pad (two at rebirth 1 and at rebirth 10), and the last four open at rebirths 20, 22, 24 and 26. Every early rebirth visibly grows the base, and before rebirth 1 a player has at most 8 pads, which keeps the start about choosing what to keep and makes merging useful (it frees two pads).
  • What a pad costs. A pad opened by rebirth R costs 25% of the next rebirth's price (a second pad at the same rebirth costs 1.5 times that). Right after a rebirth the new pad is the first thing to save for, and it takes about a quarter of the time to that next rebirth. The price follows the rebirth table by itself, so tuning one tunes both.
  • Floors stay as the look. A plot still shows floors of 8 pads; a floor appears once one of its pads is open.
PadOpens at rebirthPrice todayPrice proposed

Existing saves keep every pad they own. The rebirth costs were calibrated again with these pad prices in. A 15% price was also tried: it changed little once the rebirth costs were recalibrated, so 25% keeps pads a real purchase.

Casual players depend on the rebirth slimes. With pads tied to rebirths, more pads come only from rebirthing. A slime the next rebirth needs can't be sold or put in the vat and its label says "For rebirth", so players keep it without having to know the rule. Without that guard, casual bots that ignore the list stalled at rebirth 6 after 23 played hours; with it they reach rebirth 9 to 10.

Early rewards

Codes: WELCOME $100, LAUNCH $500 (SLIMEBOOST stays a boost). Goals keep their targets; the first rewards shrink so they help instead of skipping the start: Catch 1 $10, Catch 10 $100, Merge 1 $25, Steal 1 $50, Lock 1 $10, Unlock 6 pads $50, Unlock 8 pads $300, Earn $10K $200. The Dex milestones for 8 and 15 species pay $200 and $1,000. Late goals stay large.

Selling

Sell for 30% of what the fountain would charge for that slime, brewed bonus included. Buying and reselling always loses money, and a slime keeps real value when you replace it. Not simulated: the bots only sell to make room.

Offline income

25% instead of 50%, same 2-hour cap. Offline cash still pays toward the next rebirth (it is earned between rebirths, before the reset), so it matters, but less, and active play counts for more. The pace barely changes.

Locks and stealing

Unchanged on purpose: locks stay free and anyone can rob anyone, so the richest players are the hardest to rob. In the simulation grinders keep their base locked 98% of the time they are online and are robbed about once a day, while casual players are robbed about 17 times a week.

What the simulation can't tell us

  • Bot profiles are guesses at real behaviour. Real session lengths and retention from analytics should replace them.
  • Bots merge any three of a rarity and don't plan recipes well, so the value of merging is argued with numbers above, not measured.
  • Selling at 30%, late-rank cosmetics and a second vat were not simulated.
  • Once a player reaches rebirth 30 the goals left are the SlimeDex, bosses and stealing; the simulation can't say how long that holds them.
  • Upgrades are still maxed by rebirth 6. Letting Slime Snacks go to 25 levels with rebirth gates would keep a cash sink through rebirth 20, but needs one more calibration round, because it raises income.
  • Steal balance only moved a little: stealing still earns 2 to 4 times more income per minute than the fountain. That fits a stealing game, but watch it.

Config changes

All of it is Config plus four small code changes: per-rarity wild price (Spawner.price), the brewed bonus (Genome.baseIncome, TripleMerge, the save field), a cost table in Economy.rebirthCost, and per-pad rebirth gates and prices in Economy.padCost and PlayerData.buyPad (plus the pad prompt showing "Rebirth N" on a locked pad). Saves need a new optional brewed field on slimes; old slimes load as not brewed.



Simulator: lune run tools/livetest/pacing on the real World, GameLoop and Actions code, with an override file for each setup. 16 bots per server (8 casual, 5 average, 3 grinder) for the one-week runs; 6 casual, 5 average, 5 grinder for the 55-day runs. Medians throughout.
