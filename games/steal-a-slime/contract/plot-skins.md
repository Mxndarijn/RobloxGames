# 15. Gaps #15: plot skins unlocked by collection (2026-10-16, Archer)

**Status:** contract first. `Config.FEATURES.PlotSkins` (default **on** since 2026-10-18); `false` = no remote, no State fields, no plot attribute, no visuals. The save field is still read and written.

**Base themes (2026-10-22, Merijn):** a plot skin is a theme for the whole goo jar tower. There is one per slime family (Meadow = `classic`, Garden, Frost & Sea, Starlight) and two collections across families (Candy, Forest). A skin unlocks by collecting its slimes, not by a rarity tier.

- **Rule:** `Config.PLOT_SKINS` lists the skins. A skin names its slimes either by `family` (a `FountainTiers.FAMILIES` id: every `SlimeRoster` spawn species of that family) or by `species` (a list of `SlimeRoster` ids). It unlocks once the owner has found (SlimeDex, `data.found`) at least `ceil(#slimes * threshold)` of them (`threshold` 0.75). `classic` names no slimes and is always unlocked. `PlotSkins.slimes(skin)` resolves the list. `src/shared/PlotSkins`.
- **Skins:** `classic` (Meadow, everyone), `garden` (family `orchard`), `crystal` (Frost & Sea, family `frost`), `starlight` (family `starlight`), `candy` (Gummy, Mochi, Flan, Soda Pop, Rocky Road), `mushroom` (Forest: Mushie, Froggo, Pebble, Truffle, Moo-shroom, Bloom). Ids are permanent save ids.
- **Save (versioned since v6, §8.9c):** `plotSkin: string`. Missing, unknown or not unlocked loads as `"classic"` (`SaveData.deserialize`; `SaveData.migratePlotSkin` is the pure step; `migrateV5` normalises unknown ids). No save version bump: the unlock is recomputed from `found` on every load.
- **Remote:** `Request("EquipPlotSkin", skinId)` (`string`; rate limit 0.5 s). Refused unless the skin exists and is unlocked, or when the flag is off. Equipping `classic` always works.
- **State (flag on only):** `plotSkin: string`, `plotSkins: { { id, name, setName, tier, owned, total, needed, fraction, unlocked, equipped } }` (classic first). `setName` = the group the skin's slimes come from (the family's name, or the skin's own name for a collection), `tier` = nil, `owned` / `total` / `needed` count the skin's slimes.
- **Plot attribute (flag on only):** `Plot_<i>.PlotSkin = <id>` ("classic" for a free plot).
- **Tower look (server, `MeshSwap`):** every kit mesh slot under `Plot_<i>` shows `"<mesh>@<skin>"` instead of `<mesh>` when `MeshAssets` has that themed mesh (the same shape in the skin's colours, built to the classic piece's box), else the classic mesh. Companion meshes ride on a piece: `goo_jar_props@<skin>` on the `goo_jar_foot` slot and `goo_jar_hat@<skin>` on each `goo_jar_mascot` slot, placed from `JarMeshes` (`<name>@<skin>` boxes round the piece's anchor). Upper jars built later get the plot's skin too. When the attribute changes (claim, equip, leave), the plot's slots are swapped once `WorldMeshesReady` is set. Glass, the door ring and the crown are the same in every skin. No new world slots: `WorldLayout.build` is unchanged.
- **Client menu:** Nav key `Skins` (a tile in the left menu, after Codes) opens the Skins menu on its Base tab (the Cloud tab is [pet-seats.md](pet-seats.md)): one row per skin (its icon, name, a bar and the count toward `needed`, or Equipped) and a pane for the selected skin: its slimes (`PlotSkins.slimes`, each found or not from `State.dex`), how many unlock it, and Equip (`EquipPlotSkin`), Equipped, or a well saying how many slimes are still needed. Not part of the SlimeDex any more. While the flag is off the menu shows only its empty line.
- **Client visuals (`PlotDecor.applySkin`):** the floor colour, trim tint and decor mesh from before the jar tower. They paint parts the tower hides, so they show nothing on the tower.
- **Studio-only debug:** SlimeUI attribute `PcwPlotSkin` (skin id) forces Plot_1's skin and the picker view for shots.
