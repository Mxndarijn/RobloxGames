# Obby: the sky course

A short parkour course in its own sky world, reached through a portal in the yard, called **Obby** everywhere players see it. Clearing it earns **x1.5 income for 5 minutes**; the next run earns it again **5 minutes after the boost ends**. The course rebuilds every **10 minutes** from new sections. Design: `docs/obby-proposal.html`. Tunables: `Config.OBBY`. No feature flag (it ships on).

## 2. Rotation

`slot = floor(serverTime / ROTATION_SECONDS)`. `pick(slot)` takes one section per entry of `ORDER` (easy x2, medium x2, hard x2: 6 sections), never one twice, and none of the previous course's sections inside a chain of `CHAIN_SLOTS` rotations (the first course of a chain starts fresh). Each gets a layout 1..`LAYOUTS`. The same slot gives the same course on every server and client. A new rotation rebuilds the course once no one is running, or `REBUILD_GRACE` seconds after it starts; runners still on it are set down by the portal.

## 3. A run

- **Start**: stepping into the portal circle (`WorldLayout.OBBY.portalRadius`) from outside it. Refused while carrying a slime, while a new course is waiting to be built, or when the player already **cleared this course** (`ObbyService.cleared`: a finish that counted on the same `slot`, i.e. `session.obbyBest.slot == slot`; Notify `"You cleared this course! New one in m:ss"`, `"bad"`). A run that didn't count (invalid) does not block another try; the next rotation lets everyone in again. `session.obbyBest` is runtime only, so a rejoin forgets it. The runner appears `RESPAWN_LIFT` above the start pad (`run.enteredAt`). The run's clock starts the first server tick the runner is past the start pad's front edge (`ObbyCourse.sectionAt` > 0: over section 1 or on; `run.startedAt`, nil until then), also inside the `MOVE_GRACE` after the lift when the position is on the course; standing on the pad doesn't count. The run time (the finish, `MIN_RUN_SECONDS`, `session.obbyBest`, `ObbyFinished`) counts from `startedAt`.
- **No checkpoints.** The sections follow each other with a `LINK_LENGTH` connector between two sections: a jelly block (look `jelly`, `link = true`) in line with the path, spanning the ends of the steps on either side (at least `LINK_MIN_WIDTH`, plus `LINK_MARGIN` each side), so it reads as part of the course, not a pad. Every section starts and ends with a jump. Each section owns a span along the course (`Course.spans`: `from`, `to`, `floor` = the lower of its entry and exit heights), the connector after it included.
- **Section progress** (invisible): every tick the server finds the part of the course the runner is over (`ObbyCourse.sectionAt`: 0 = start pad, 1..5 = sections, 6 = finish pad) and keeps the furthest one, in order (`run.section`), and the one they are over now (`run.at`, 0 after a fall back to the start pad). Getting past a section without ever being over it marks the run invalid.
- **Fall**: the runner's root more than `FALL_DEPTH` under the lowest platform they could still land on (`ObbyCourse.fallHeight`: the lowest top, at its lowest while it moves, of every solid non-obstacle part within `FALL_REACH` along the course, `Course.floors`) goes straight back to the start pad, `RESPAWN_LIFT` above it. The client does it the same frame for the local player (`ObbyFall`: pivots the character, zeroes its velocity, a white ColorCorrection flash). The server sees the jump back onto the start pad (more than `ObbyService.RESET_JUMP` studs back in one tick: `Result.resets`, the anti-cheat's server-move window opens and `ObbyFell` is tracked) and, as the backup, moves the runner itself past `FALL_DEPTH + SERVER_FALL_SLACK`. Bounces, fans, climbs and drops stay above the floors they can land on, so they never reset. The clock keeps running, the sections already done stay done, and no boost is lost. `ObbyFell` is tracked with the section the runner fell from. Checks wait `MOVE_GRACE` after every move the server makes.
- **Goo Ring home**: the yard's Goo Ring at `HOME.scale` (0.72) stands on the start pad's back edge (`HOME_AT` = 26 behind where the sections' x starts, a long full-width apron; a runner appears in the pad's middle, 18 studs in front of the ring, so the camera behind them never looks at the ring's back) facing along the course, so a runner pops out of it facing the sections; "BACK TO BASE" is painted on the start pad in front of it. The start pad ends flush with the ring's back face (`HOME_DEPTH` behind its middle), so the floor stops where the ring does. Walking back into the ring (`ObbyCourse.inHome`: the circle `Course.home`, radius `HOME_RADIUS`, `HOME_BACK` behind the ring's middle) ends the run and sets the runner down at `exit` by the portal; no fall is tracked.
- **Off course** (more than `ObbyService.OFF_COURSE` studs past the ends or sides), death or no character: the run ends.
- **Finish**: standing on the finish pad (`ObbyCourse.onFinish`, within `STAND_HEIGHT` above its top). The run time, a best on this course (`session.obbyBest`), and `ObbyRules.grant` when ready. Then the player is set down at `exit` by the portal. An invalid run (a skipped section, or a finish faster than `MIN_RUN_SECONDS`) still finishes but earns nothing and sets no best.
- Runs during the boost or the cooldown are allowed (on a course not cleared yet); they earn no boost. The two rules don't overlap: the boost cooldown never refuses entry, it only decides whether a finish earns the boost; the cleared rule refuses entry until the next course. A finish that earns the boost is ready again 10 min later (`BOOST_SECONDS + COOLDOWN_SECONDS`), while the next course comes at most `ROTATION_SECONDS` (10 min) later, so a runner can be back on the new course while the boost is still cooling down: that run earns no boost.
- **Lock at half speed**: while a run is on (portal entry to finish, Goo Ring, off course, rotation, death / no character, leaving the game), the runner's base lock counts down at `OBBY.LOCK_RATE` (0.5: it loses 0.5 s per real second). The lock stays one end time: each obby tick (`ServerLoop`, 0.1 s) `ObbyLock.step` pushes `session.lockUntil` out by the part of the tick the lock did not use, from the run's last tick (`run.lockAt`, else `enteredAt`: from the portal, not the clock), so the plot's `LockUntil` attribute, every client countdown (`LockTimerTag`, the lock card, the lock pad ring, the door), the steal checks and other players' views all show the slower lock with no client change. A lock that starts mid-run counts from `lockStartedAt`; one that runs out mid-run ends when it would at the slow rate and is never revived; no lock or an expired one is left alone; the breather lock keeps its `breatherUntil` mark (it moves with `lockUntil`). The rate is held within `ObbyLock.MIN_RATE` (0.1)..1, so a run never stops a lock. The session is marked dirty when the shown whole second changes (`State.lockUntil`). Locks are runtime only, so nothing new is saved.
- **Standard avatar**: the course is tuned for a standard avatar, so during a run an R15 runner wears `OBBY.AVATAR_SCALES` (height, width, depth and head 1; body type and proportion 0). `server/ObbyAvatar` (pure) decides, `server/ObbyAvatarView` writes the Humanoid's scale NumberValues each obby tick between `ObbyService.step` and the teleports, so the runner is resized before the lift onto the start pad. It keeps only the player's own (non-standard) values, normalises a character that respawns mid-run again, and puts the values back once when the run ends, on the character it normalised. R6 and characters with `AutomaticScalingEnabled` off are left alone.

## 4. Save and State

- `PlayerData.obbyUntil` / `obbyReadyAt` (server time, 0 = never), saved as `obby = { untilAt, readyAt }` without a version bump (`SaveData`, missing = never earned). `obbyReadyAt = obbyUntil + COOLDOWN_SECONDS`.
- `data.obbyBonus` is runtime only: `ObbyService.refresh` (every `GameLoop.step`) sets it from the timers, so it survives a rejoin. `Perks.bonusMultiplier` multiplies `1 + obbyBonus` with the other bonuses.
- `session.obbyRun = { slot, section, at, enteredAt, startedAt?, invalid?, settleUntil?, lastX?, lockAt?, vine? }` is runtime only. A new run starts at `section = 0`, `at = 0`, no `startedAt`.
- `State.obby` (`Types.ObbyView`): `bonus`, `endsAt?` (while boosted), `readyAt?` (until ready), `sections` (names), `rotatesAt`, `run? = { section, at, of, startedAt?, lockRate }` (`section` = furthest section reached, 0 = start pad; `at` = the section the runner is over now, 0 = start pad, `of + 1` = finish pad; `startedAt` = when the clock started, nil while still on the start pad; `lockRate` = `OBBY.LOCK_RATE`, how fast the runner's base lock counts down meanwhile), `best?` (set = this course is cleared: the portal sign's status line says "Cleared! Next course soon" and the portal refuses entry). The run card (`Layout.OBBY_CARD`, top centre in the banner strip, under the banner stack) has three rows: `Start` on the start pad, `Section x/6` (the section the runner is over now, `run.at`, not the furthest; the count is `run.of` = `#Course.spans`), `Finish` on the finish pad, with the run clock (`0:00.0`, standing still, until `run.startedAt` arrives); one pip per section from where the runner is (behind them: a check, the one they are on: a mint ring on a light fill, the furthest reached this run when a fall put them behind it: a mint ring on the dark well, still to come: an Ink well); and the base lock from `State.lockUntil`, `🔒 m:ss` with a `50% slower` chip (from `lockRate`; none at rate 1), or `🔓 Base open` with no lock running. The server sends lockUntil only now and then while it pushes it out, so the card counts down at `lockRate` from the last value it saw and never steps back up (`ObbyHud.lockLeft`). No new remote or attribute.
- HUD (bottom-right status row, `HudClusters.obby`): while boosted, the slot shows the time left to `endsAt`. After it, until `readyAt`, the same slot shows kind `"cooldown"`: the icon under an Ink veil, the timer muted, tooltip "Next boost ready in m:ss". Once ready the slot goes away (no "Ready!" flash; the portal sign says it). A cooldown slot is the first to give way when the row is full, before the featured Secret.
- No remote for the run: the server reads positions. The one request is `ObbyVine` (§5 "Vine ropes for everyone"), looks only.

## 5. World

- `WorldLayout.OBBY`: the portal in the lawn lane between plots 8 and 1 (bearing 337.5, r 155), in front of the yard's windmill scene. `PropClearance` keeps `clearRadius` round the portal clear.
- **Path**: a star walk (`WorldLayout.obbyPath`, the Hall of Fame path's style, with goo-green dots in the stars' places) from the ring path's outer rail out to the portal's back face (`portalBack` past its centre, so the ring stands on the path's end), `pathWidth` wide, and the `Constants.OBBY_ARCH` arch ("OBBY", `Props.obbyArch`, a `PROP_SPOTS` entry) over it `archDistance` from the map centre, just past the ritual circle (`Config.RITUAL`) beside its plaza end. `PropClearance` keeps the path clear ("Obby path"), so the yard dressing (`YardProps`) and the plaza props stand off it.
- **Obby world**: the course stands in its own zone, `WorldLayout.OBBY.world`, thousands of studs from the map (past any view distance and the Atmosphere), so nothing of it shows from the plots, plaza or bases and nothing of the map shows from it. The course frame is `world.origin` / `world.yaw`. `WorldLayout.inObbyWorld(pos)` is true inside `world.radius` of the zone's centre.
- The backdrop (`visuals/client/ObbyBackdrop`, from `WorldLayout.obbyBackdrop()`, a `Constants.OBBY_BACKDROP` Folder in workspace): floating goo island meshes (three kinds in two rows, two big landmark kinds far out), cumulus cloud meshes and a cloud sea of cloud-bank meshes at staggered heights over a flat base disc, from `shared/modeling-kit/models/obby_*.py`, loaded with `CreateMeshPartAsync` once per client (plain parts only if a mesh fails). Client only: `ObbyWorld` builds it when the local player arrives and removes it after they leave, so the server world and its part budget never carry it. Decor only: no collision, no queries, no touches.
- **Vine ropes for everyone**: every client shows the same ropes. A rope nobody holds swings on server time (`ObbyFx`, `workspace:GetServerTimeNow()`), so its angle is the same on every client. The rope a runner grabs follows their own pendulum on their client (`ObbyMoves`, `Vine.HELD_ANGLE`); their client sends it as Request `ObbyVine` (the knob's `ObbyId`, `""` on a let go; only the latest, one at a time, at most every 0.1 s, again after 1 s if not shown: `ObbyVineSend`). The server keeps it on the run when the id is a vine rope of the standing course (`ObbyService.holdVine`; `run.vine`, runtime only) and sets the Player's `ObbyVine` attribute at once and every view tick (`PlayerAttributes.syncObbyVine`, `ObbyService.heldVine`: nil once the run ends or the course rotates). Every other client (`ObbyVineSync`) turns that rope to the angle the holder's replicated root hangs at (`Vine.angleAt`, written as `Vine.OTHER_ANGLE` / `OTHER_FADE`; the local player's own hold wins), raises the holder's arms on the knob and runs the rope Beam to their hands (`ObbyVinePose`; joint edits never replicate, so each client poses the others itself), and lets the rope swing on and ease back over `ObbyMoves.RETURN` after a let go. A holder whose root is further than the rope's length plus `Vine.HOLD_SLACK` from its pivot is not drawn on it.
- Banner fade (`visuals/client/ObbySignFade`, pure rules in `visuals/shared/ObbyBannerFade`): on the local client, a picture banner fades out over `Config.OBBY.BANNER_FADE.SECONDS` once the runner's root is `HIDE_PAST` studs past it along the course, so it never blocks the camera behind them, and fades back in once they are back within `SHOW_PAST` (walking back or a reset). Per client only; other players' views are their own.
- The portal moves the runner to the start pad in the obby world; a finish, a rotation or leaving the course sets them down at `exit` by the portal, facing the plaza with the portal behind them (`exitYaw`), so the camera lands behind them looking where they go. Every server move lifts the root by how much taller the character stands than a standard avatar (`ObbyService.landing`), so a runner whose own body scales just came back lands on their feet. A player in the obby world without a run (off course, a cancelled run) is set down at `exit` on the next tick.
- `visuals/client/ObbyWorld`: while the local player's root is `inObbyWorld`, it swaps to `ObbyLook` (Lighting, Atmosphere, a ColorCorrection tint, and the place's Bloom and SunRays turned down) on the frame they arrive, and back on the frame they leave: at once, in the same frame as the camera cut (no veil, fade or tween), with the camera set behind the player at the same zoom and pitch, facing the way they face (also when an obby move held it Scriptable). The way back restores the map's look exactly (NightSky recomputes it from the day / night and weather values; the weather grade snaps back instead of fading in). The boss card hides while the player is there. `NightSky` blends the look on top of the day / night and weather look; the look has its own day and night values (`ObbyLook.LOOK` / `NIGHT`, by `NightLook.factor`). Course parts use no Neon.
- `ObbyLook.isInside` (set by `ObbyWorld` each frame, `onInsideChanged` for changes) is the one "in the obby world" signal on the client. While it is true the home plot marker (beacon, tag, edge arrow) hides, the weather (`WeatherFx`) and Slime Rain draw nothing, and the tutorial guide computes no paths (`GuideView` `shouldPath`) and hides its edge pointer. All of it comes back on the way out.
- `workspace.Obby.Course` (attribute `Slot`) is **client-only**, like the backdrop: the server publishes the slot (`workspace.Obby` attribute `Constants.OBBY_SLOT`, set by `ObbyView.build` each rotation) and every client builds the same course from it (`ObbyCourseView`, `ObbyParts`), so the server world and its runtime part budget carry no course parts whatever the rotation (`tests/RuntimeParts.spec` walks the slots and checks it). Each player's character collides with their own copy. It holds one Part per `ObbyCourse.Part` (a TrussPart for ladders), plus the Goo Ring home (`ObbyParts.gooRing`: invisible MeshAsset slots `HomeGooRing` and `HomeGooSwirl`, filled with the kit meshes on the client by `ObbyCourseView`, and `HomeLabel` with a SurfaceGui on its top: no collision, queries or touches). Moving parts carry `Constants.OBBY_*` attributes (motion kind, rest CFrame, axis, amplitude, period, phase, delay, away, duty, min, shrink axes, power, arm). Motion kinds: slide, spin, melt, flip, shrink, bounce (trampolines and cannons throw the local player up at `power`), lift (wind columns), swing (pendulums), rock (seesaws), roll (logs rolling round their length), punch (out fast, hold, back), gust (a wind zone that blows the local player sideways while on). Belts have a surface velocity, ice no friction.
- Slime Guard opens its launch window for players on a run (`SlimeGuardView`): bounces and fans rise faster than a jump.
- `workspace.Obby.Portal`: the Goo Ring (`ObbyParts.gooRing`: MeshAsset slots `GooRing` = `obby_goo_ring_eyes`, a slime ring with eyes rising out of a goo puddle, and `GooSwirl` = `obby_goo_swirl`, a vortex that turns on each client, `Constants.GOO_SPIN` / `GOO_PIVOT`, played by `ObbyFx`, with a purple PointLight and sparkles drifting in), filled by `MeshSwap.applyUnder` from init.server; and a goo-topped signpost beside it (`SignPost` = `obby_goo_sign`) whose board carries `Sign`, an invisible part with a SurfaceGui on both faces (no light column: the path and the OBBY arch lead players there) (`Title` "OBBY", `Constants.OBBY_SIGN_STATUS`, `Constants.OBBY_SIGN_COURSE`), MaxDistance 200 (guides/roblox-world-labels.md). A mesh that fails to load leaves its slot empty; the walk-in circle still works.

## 6. Analytics

`ObbyStarted`, `ObbyFinished` (value = seconds; field: boost earned / cooling / invalid), `ObbyFell` (field: the section the runner fell from; the runner goes back to the start pad).
