# Obby: the sky course

A short parkour course in its own sky world, reached through a portal in the yard, called **Obby** everywhere players see it. Clearing it earns **x1.5 income for 5 minutes**; the next run earns it again **5 minutes after the boost ends**. The course rebuilds every **30 minutes** from new sections, the same course on every server, and every course has one **fastest-time board** shared by all servers (§7); retries are allowed to beat your time. Design: `docs/obby-proposal.html`, the shared course and board `docs/obby-shared-course-proposal.html`. Tunables: `Config.OBBY`. No feature flag (it ships on).

## 2. Rotation

`slot = floor(serverTime / ROTATION_SECONDS)` (30 min; `CHAIN_SLOTS` = 48, still a day). `pick(slot)` takes one section per entry of `ORDER` (easy x2, medium x2, hard x2: 6 sections), never one twice, and none of the previous course's sections inside a chain of `CHAIN_SLOTS` rotations (the first course of a chain starts fresh). Each gets a layout 1..`LAYOUTS`. The same slot gives the same course on every server and client. **Course ID**: the slot plus `ObbyCourse.hash(course)`, 8 hex characters over every built part (size, position, rotation, look, motion and every other field, numbers rounded to 0.01) and `OBBY.COURSE_VERSION`, so a server on other section code (a rolling update) builds another hash and posts to another board. Players see the course number `ObbyRules.courseNumber(slot)` = slot - `OBBY.COURSE_EPOCH` + 1 ("Course #1,204"); the hash is never shown. A new rotation rebuilds the course once no one is running, or `REBUILD_GRACE` seconds after it starts; runners still on it are set down by the portal.

## 3. A run

- **Start**: stepping into the portal circle (`WorldLayout.OBBY.portalRadius`) from outside it. Refused while carrying a slime or while a new course is waiting to be built. A cleared course lets you in again (retries chase your best time): `ObbyService.cleared` (a counted finish on the same `slot`, `session.obbyBest.slot == slot`) only marks the run a retry in analytics. The runner appears `RESPAWN_LIFT` above the start pad (`run.enteredAt`). The run's clock starts the first server tick the runner is past the start pad's front edge (`ObbyCourse.sectionAt` > 0: over section 1 or on; `run.startedAt`, nil until then), also inside the `MOVE_GRACE` after the lift when the position is on the course; standing on the pad doesn't count. The run time (the finish, `MIN_RUN_SECONDS`, `session.obbyBest`, the board, `ObbyFinished`) counts from `startedAt`.
- **No checkpoints.** The sections follow each other with a `LINK_LENGTH` connector between two sections: a jelly block (look `jelly`, `link = true`) in line with the path, spanning the ends of the steps on either side (at least `LINK_MIN_WIDTH`, plus `LINK_MARGIN` each side), so it reads as part of the course, not a pad. Every section starts and ends with a jump. Each section owns a span along the course (`Course.spans`: `from`, `to`, `floor` = the lower of its entry and exit heights), the connector after it included.
- **Section progress** (invisible): every tick the server finds the part of the course the runner is over (`ObbyCourse.sectionAt`: 0 = start pad, 1..5 = sections, 6 = finish pad) and keeps the furthest one, in order (`run.section`), and the one they are over now (`run.at`, 0 after a fall back to the start pad). Getting past a section without ever being over it marks the run invalid.
- **Fall**: the runner's root more than `FALL_DEPTH` under the lowest platform they could still land on (`ObbyCourse.fallHeight`: the lowest top, at its lowest while it moves, of every solid non-obstacle part within `FALL_REACH` along the course, `Course.floors`) goes straight back to the start pad, `RESPAWN_LIFT` above it. The client does it the same frame for the local player (`ObbyFall`: pivots the character, zeroes its velocity, a white ColorCorrection flash). The server sees the jump back onto the start pad (more than `ObbyService.RESET_JUMP` studs back in one tick: `Result.resets`, the anti-cheat's server-move window opens and `ObbyFell` is tracked) and, as the backup, moves the runner itself past `FALL_DEPTH + SERVER_FALL_SLACK`. Bounces, fans, climbs and drops stay above the floors they can land on, so they never reset. The clock keeps running, the sections already done stay done, and no boost is lost. `ObbyFell` is tracked with the section the runner fell from. Checks wait `MOVE_GRACE` after every move the server makes.
- **Goo Ring home**: the yard's Goo Ring at `HOME.scale` (0.72) stands on the start pad's back edge (`HOME_AT` = 26 behind where the sections' x starts, a long full-width apron; a runner appears in the pad's middle, 18 studs in front of the ring, so the camera behind them never looks at the ring's back) facing along the course, so a runner pops out of it facing the sections; "BACK TO BASE" is painted on the start pad in front of it. The start pad ends flush with the ring's back face (`HOME_DEPTH` behind its middle), so the floor stops where the ring does. Walking back into the ring (`ObbyCourse.inHome`: the circle `Course.home`, radius `HOME_RADIUS`, `HOME_BACK` behind the ring's middle) ends the run and sets the runner down at `exit` by the portal; no fall is tracked.
- **Off course** (more than `ObbyService.OFF_COURSE` studs past the ends or sides), death or no character: the run ends.
- **Finish**: standing on the finish pad (`ObbyCourse.onFinish`, within `STAND_HEIGHT` above its top). The run time in tenths (`ObbyRules.tenths`), a best on this course (`session.obbyBest`, also read back from the board after a rejoin) when it passes the board rules (§7) and beats the player's own best, and `ObbyRules.grant` when ready. Then the player is set down at `exit` by the portal. Notify: `"Cleared in 0:44.9! New best: #6 on this course"` (a new best), `"Cleared in 0:52.0. Your best: 0:44.9 (#6)"` (a slower retry), with `" · x1.5 income for 5 min"` when it earned the boost; a run that counts but can't post (§7) gets `"Cleared in 0:52.0! Boost ready again in m:ss"` (or the boost line). An invalid run (a skipped section, or a finish faster than `MIN_RUN_SECONDS`) still finishes but earns nothing and sets no best.
- **Boost**: unchanged, on its own timer: any counted finish earns it when `ObbyRules` says it is ready, first try or retry; runs during the boost or the cooldown earn no boost. The cooldown never refuses entry.
- **Same speed for everyone**: during a run the runner walks at `Config.BASE_WALK_SPEED` (`Carry.targetWalkSpeed`): no Speed Coil, no Boss Champion speed, no PvP or boss-goo slows, no PvP freeze (WorldView). The normal speed comes back on the first tick after the run ends.
- **Lock at half speed** (every run, retries too): while a run is on (portal entry to finish, Goo Ring, off course, rotation, death / no character, leaving the game), the runner's base lock counts down at `OBBY.LOCK_RATE` (0.5: it loses 0.5 s per real second). The lock stays one end time: each obby tick (`ServerLoop`, 0.1 s) `ObbyLock.step` pushes `session.lockUntil` out by the part of the tick the lock did not use, from the run's last tick (`run.lockAt`, else `enteredAt`: from the portal, not the clock), so the plot's `LockUntil` attribute, every client countdown (`LockTimerTag`, the lock card, the lock pad ring, the door), the steal checks and other players' views all show the slower lock with no client change. A lock that starts mid-run counts from `lockStartedAt`; one that runs out mid-run ends when it would at the slow rate and is never revived; no lock or an expired one is left alone; the breather lock keeps its `breatherUntil` mark (it moves with `lockUntil`). The rate is held within `ObbyLock.MIN_RATE` (0.1)..1, so a run never stops a lock. The session is marked dirty when the shown whole second changes (`State.lockUntil`). Locks are runtime only, so nothing new is saved.
- **Standard avatar**: the course is tuned for a standard avatar, so during a run an R15 runner wears `OBBY.AVATAR_SCALES` (height, width, depth and head 1; body type and proportion 0). `server/ObbyAvatar` (pure) decides, `server/ObbyAvatarView` writes the Humanoid's scale NumberValues each obby tick between `ObbyService.step` and the teleports, so the runner is resized before the lift onto the start pad. It keeps only the player's own (non-standard) values, normalises a character that respawns mid-run again, and puts the values back once when the run ends, on the character it normalised. R6 and characters with `AutomaticScalingEnabled` off are left alone.

## 4. Save and State

- `PlayerData.obbyUntil` / `obbyReadyAt` (server time, 0 = never), saved as `obby = { untilAt, readyAt }` without a version bump (`SaveData`, missing = never earned). `obbyReadyAt = obbyUntil + COOLDOWN_SECONDS`.
- `data.obbyBonus` is runtime only: `ObbyService.refresh` (every `GameLoop.step`) sets it from the timers, so it survives a rejoin. `Perks.bonusMultiplier` multiplies `1 + obbyBonus` with the other bonuses.
- `session.obbyRun = { slot, section, at, enteredAt, startedAt?, invalid?, settleUntil?, lastX?, lockAt?, vine? }` is runtime only. A new run starts at `section = 0`, `at = 0`, no `startedAt`.
- `State.obby` (`Types.ObbyView`): `bonus`, `endsAt?` (while boosted), `readyAt?` (until ready), `sections` (names), `rotatesAt`, `run? = { section, at, of, startedAt?, lockRate }` (`section` = furthest section reached, 0 = start pad; `at` = the section the runner is over now, 0 = start pad, `of + 1` = finish pad; `startedAt` = when the clock started, nil while still on the start pad; `lockRate` = `OBBY.LOCK_RATE`, how fast the runner's base lock counts down meanwhile), `best?` (the best counted finish on this course, seconds in whole tenths), `rank?` (that best's rank on the course board, `BOARD.RANK_DEPTH + 1` = deeper; nil before the board has it), `course` (the course number). The sign board's lines: the boost status (`"Step in: x1.5 income!"`, `"x1.5 income · 3:12 left"`, `"Boost ready in 1:40"`) and `"Course #1,204 · 12:34 left"`. The run card (`Layout.OBBY_CARD`, top centre in the banner strip, under the banner stack) has these rows: `Start` on the start pad, `Section x/6` (the section the runner is over now, `run.at`, not the furthest; the count is `run.of` = `#Course.spans`), `Finish` on the finish pad, with the run clock (`0:00.0`, standing still, until `run.startedAt` arrives); one pip per section from where the runner is (behind them: a check, the one they are on: a mint ring on a light fill, the furthest reached this run when a fall put them behind it: a mint ring on the dark well, still to come: an Ink well); once you have a time on this course `"Best 0:51.4"` with a gold rank chip `"#23"` (`ObbyHud.bestText`; the card grows by `bestH`); and the base lock from `State.lockUntil`, `🔒 m:ss` with a `50% slower locks` chip (under the time on cards too narrow for both) (from `lockRate`; none at rate 1), or `🔓 Base open` with no lock running. The server sends lockUntil only now and then while it pushes it out, so the card counts down at `lockRate` from the last value it saw and never steps back up (`ObbyHud.lockLeft`). No new remote or attribute.
- HUD (bottom-right status row, `HudClusters.obby`): while boosted, the slot shows the time left to `endsAt`. After it, until `readyAt`, the same slot shows kind `"cooldown"`: the icon under an Ink veil, the timer muted, tooltip "Next boost ready in m:ss". Once ready the slot goes away (no "Ready!" flash; the portal sign says it). A cooldown slot is the first to give way when the row is full, before the featured Secret.
- No remote for the run: the server reads positions. The one request is `ObbyVine` (§5 "Vine ropes for everyone"), looks only.

## 5. World

- `WorldLayout.OBBY`: the portal in the lawn lane between plots 8 and 1 (bearing 337.5, r 155), in front of the yard's windmill scene. `PropClearance` keeps `clearRadius` round the portal clear.
- **Path**: a star walk (`WorldLayout.obbyPath`, the Hall of Fame path's style, with goo-green dots in the stars' places) from the ring path's outer rail out to the portal's back face (`portalBack` past its centre, so the ring stands on the path's end), `pathWidth` wide, and the `Constants.OBBY_ARCH` arch ("OBBY", `Props.obbyArch`, a `PROP_SPOTS` entry) over it `archDistance` from the map centre, just past the ritual circle (`Config.RITUAL`) beside its plaza end. `PropClearance` keeps the path clear ("Obby path"), so the yard dressing (`YardProps`) and the plaza props stand off it.
- **Obby world**: the course stands in its own zone, `WorldLayout.OBBY.world`, thousands of studs from the map (past any view distance and the Atmosphere), so nothing of it shows from the plots, plaza or bases and nothing of the map shows from it. The course frame is `world.origin` / `world.yaw`. `WorldLayout.inObbyWorld(pos)` is true inside `world.radius` of the zone's centre.
- The backdrop (`visuals/client/ObbyBackdrop`, from `WorldLayout.obbyBackdrop()`, a `Constants.OBBY_BACKDROP` Folder in workspace): floating goo island meshes (three kinds in two rows, two big landmark kinds far out), cumulus cloud meshes and a cloud sea of cloud-bank meshes at staggered heights over a flat base disc, from `shared/modeling-kit/models/obby_*.py`, loaded with `CreateMeshPartAsync` once per client (plain parts only if a mesh fails). Client only: `ObbyWorld` builds it when the local player arrives and removes it after they leave, so the server world and its part budget never carry it. Decor only: no collision, no queries, no touches.
- **Vine ropes for everyone**: every client shows the same ropes. A rope nobody holds swings on server time (`ObbyFx`, `workspace:GetServerTimeNow()`), so its angle is the same on every client. The rope a runner grabs follows their own pendulum on their client (`ObbyMoves`, `Vine.HELD_ANGLE`); their client sends it as Request `ObbyVine` (the knob's `ObbyId`, `""` on a let go; only the latest, one at a time, at most every 0.1 s, again after 1 s if not shown: `ObbyVineSend`). The server keeps it on the run when the id is a vine rope of the standing course (`ObbyService.holdVine`; `run.vine`, runtime only) and sets the Player's `ObbyVine` attribute at once and every view tick (`PlayerAttributes.syncObbyVine`, `ObbyService.heldVine`: nil once the run ends or the course rotates). Every other client (`ObbyVineSync`) turns that rope to the angle the holder's replicated root hangs at (`Vine.angleAt`, written as `Vine.OTHER_ANGLE` / `OTHER_FADE`; the local player's own hold wins), raises the holder's arms on the knob and runs the rope Beam to their hands (`ObbyVinePose`; joint edits never replicate, so each client poses the others itself), and lets the rope swing on and ease back over `ObbyMoves.RETURN` after a let go. A holder whose root is further than the rope's length plus `Vine.HOLD_SLACK` from its pivot is not drawn on it.
- Banner fade (`visuals/client/ObbySignFade`, pure rules in `visuals/shared/ObbyBannerFade`): on the local client, a picture banner fades out over `Config.OBBY.BANNER_FADE.SECONDS` once the runner's root is `HIDE_PAST` studs past it along the course, so it never blocks the camera behind them, and fades back in once they are back within `SHOW_PAST` (walking back or a reset). Per client only; other players' views are their own.
- Camera guard (`visuals/client/ObbyCameraGuard`, pure rules in `visuals/shared/ObbyCameraClip`): Roblox's camera only stops at parts that collide, so in the obby world the local client also stops it in front of a non-collide course part (a lava floor): each frame, after the camera module, where the line from the camera's focus to the camera enters such a part, the camera moves to `Config.OBBY.CAMERA_CLIP.MARGIN` in front of that face. A part counts when it is a plain Part (not a MeshPart or special mesh), not hidden on this client, under `MAX_TRANSPARENCY`, at least `MIN_THICK` on every side and with a largest face of at least `MIN_FACE` square studs; a part the focus is inside doesn't count.
- The portal moves the runner to the start pad in the obby world; a finish, a rotation or leaving the course sets them down at `exit` by the portal, facing the plaza with the portal behind them (`exitYaw`), so the camera lands behind them looking where they go. Every server move lifts the root by how much taller the character stands than a standard avatar (`ObbyService.landing`), so a runner whose own body scales just came back lands on their feet. A player in the obby world without a run (off course, a cancelled run) is set down at `exit` on the next tick.
- `visuals/client/ObbyWorld`: while the local player's root is `inObbyWorld`, it swaps to `ObbyLook` (Lighting, Atmosphere, a ColorCorrection tint, and the place's Bloom and SunRays turned down) on the frame they arrive, and back on the frame they leave: at once, in the same frame as the camera cut (no veil, fade or tween), with the camera set behind the player at the same zoom and pitch, facing the way they face (also when an obby move held it Scriptable). Course parts use no Neon.

## 6. Analytics

`ObbyStarted` (field: `Run - First try` / `Run - Retry`, a retry = the player already has a counted finish on this course), `ObbyFinished` (value = seconds; field: boost earned / cooling / invalid), `ObbyNewBest` (a new best posted to the board; value = its rank, 0 = past #100; field: `Rank - Top 3` / `Top 10` / `Top 100` / `Deeper`), `ObbyFell` (field: the section the runner fell from; the runner goes back to the start pad).

## 7. The course board

One fastest-time board per course, shared by every server (`server/ObbyBoard`, pure; `server/ObbyBoardView`, adapter; the shared `PeriodBoard` core in shared/luau). Tunables: `Config.OBBY.BOARD` (`REFRESH`, `SIZE`, `RANK_DEPTH`, `EXPIRE_AFTER`), `COURSE_VERSION`, `COURSE_EPOCH`.

- **Store**: a MemoryStore sorted map per course, `Obby_<slot>_<hash>` (§2). Key `"u_<UserId>"`, value `{ score, at, name, hidden }`: `score` = the time in tenths, `at` = seconds into the course it was set, `name` = the display name when it was posted (every server shows the same name without lookups), `hidden` = "Show me on leaderboards" off. Sort key `score × 10⁵ + at`: lower wins, equal times go to whoever set it first. Entries expire `EXPIRE_AFTER` (1 h) after the course ends, so nothing needs cleaning up. In memory mode (`DataStoreMode = "Memory"`), in Studio, or when MemoryStoreService can't open a map the board is in memory and ranks this server only (`ObbyBoardStatus` `"Memory"`); the store is passed in as `ctx.obbyBoardStore`.
- **When a time counts** (the client never sends a time; the server posts only when all hold): the run started at this server's portal on this course's slot and is timed on server time; every section was passed in order; at least `MIN_RUN_SECONDS`; it finished within the course's 30 min plus `REBUILD_GRACE` and posts to its own slot's board (`ObbyBoard.eligible` "late"); no movement flag since the run started (`SlimeGuard.report` stamps `GuardEntry.flaggedAt`; "flagged"); the session can save (`World.canSave`: not read-only, not a Studio fallback profile; "unsaved"); and it beats the player's own best on this course. At most one write per finish: `UpdateAsync` that keeps the better entry (two servers can't overwrite a better time), tried again after 1, 2 and 4 s; a write that still fails stays queued for the next flush.
- **Reads**: every `REFRESH` (30) s and at once on a new course, the top `RANK_DEPTH` (100) of the current board, plus the last course's #1 once per course. A new best on this server is placed in the local copy at once (the board repaints within a second) and the next read confirms it. A read gives a rejoined or server-hopped player their best back (`session.obbyBest`), sends a "Show me on leaderboards" change for an entry on the current course (same time, new hidden flag), and marks every session whose rank changed (State.obby `rank`).
- **World board** (`workspace.Obby.Board`, `ObbyBoardView.build`): a Hall of Fame board (`Props.leaderboardBoard`, its `phoenix_top_earners_board` skin swapped in by MeshSwap) at `WorldLayout.OBBY.board` (`obbyBoard()`: on the other side of the ring from the sign board, turned towards the path, scale 0.8; `PropClearance` keeps its `clear` circle free), facing the path. Title bar `"FASTEST RUNS"` over `"Course #1,204 · new course in 12:34"` (server, every second), the top `SIZE` (10) rows (`BoardFace.row`, the top three on medal cards; hidden players as `"Hidden player"`, no userId ever goes to a client), the viewer's own line (each client, `ObbyHud.boardYou`: their top-10 row in the viewer's mint, else `"You #23  0:51.4"`), and the footer `"Last course: GooGoblin 0:36.8"` (`"Board offline"` while the store fails, keeping the last rows). SurfaceGui, 40 px per stud, MaxDistance 200 (guides/roblox-world-labels.md registry).
- **Status**: workspace `ObbyBoardStatus` `"Ok"` | `"Memory"` | `"Error"`, a `warn` when it turns `"Error"`.
- Not in v1 (decided): a permanent record ("Courses cleared", obby wins); an "Obby" tab in the Leaderboards panel; a per-course "too fast" floor (look at the `ObbyNewBest` top-10 times after a week first).
