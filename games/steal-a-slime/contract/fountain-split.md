# 28. Fountain split: PCD tower pieces (`FEATURES.FountainSplit`, default `false`, draft for Merijn's collision choice)
- **Flag off = nothing changes.** No piece mesh is loaded, preloaded or placed; `MeshSwap`, the colliders and the budgets are as before. Not in `FeatureOverride.FLAGS` (a boot-time look flag would flip the fountain for a whole pc-watcher run); `scripts/test-flags` forces it like every flag and `tests/ForcedFlags` lists the specs that pin the single mesh.
- **Pieces.** `MeshAssets.FOUNTAIN_SPLIT` = `fountain_split_basin / dome / eave / silo / slide` (entries carry `size` and `offset`: bbox centre minus the whole mesh's bbox centre, slot axes (kit x, kit z, -kit y), rotated with the slot). Cut from the shipped `slime_fountain.fbx` by `shared/modeling-kit/models/slime_fountain_split.py` (same faces, normals, colours; tris sum to 6354). `MeshAssets.COLLISION` makes basin, dome, eave and silo `PreciseConvexDecomposition`; the slide piece is decor.
- **MeshSwap** (`swapFountainSplit`, fountain slot only, flag on): create all 5 first, then place them (same fit and `IMPORT_YAW` as the single mesh, children of `Map.Volcano.FountainMesh` named after the piece), then turn the stand-ins and every `MeshCollider` part (Basin / Trim / Silo / Top) non-colliding. Any piece failing = nothing placed, the single mesh and colliders as without the flag. `SlideColliders` (FountainColliders) are never touched.
- Details, budgets, what Phoenix runs: `docs/FOUNTAIN_SPLIT.md`. Not shippable before a Studio walk + the ball-drop numbers (pc-watcher shot `191-fountain-split-drop`).
